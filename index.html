About Me

Who I Am

I'm Ahmed! I'm a Junior Frontend Developer and freelance web developer based in Egypt.

My journey into frontend development started at Delta University, where I discovered that a few lines of code could turn an idea into something people actually see and use. What began as curiosity during my IT studies quickly became the work I enjoy most.

One project I'm genuinely proud of is Foodie's — a food delivery app where I didn't stop at making it look good. I compressed images, added lazy loading, and split fonts, and those changes measurably improved its Lighthouse score, LCP, and TBT.

So why work with me? Because I treat every project like it's my own — I debug patiently and thoroughly until everything works correctly, and I care about the details most people skip. Take a look at my projects below, and reach out if you'd like to build something together!
